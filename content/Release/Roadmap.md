# Roadmap — WoW Forever

All dates [CONFIRMED] from the roadmap slide unless marked.

| Date | What |
|---|---|
| **17 Sep – 21 Oct 2026** | **Beta**, level 30 cap. Pre-purchase gated (Epic pack and above). |
| **17 Sep 2026, 10:00 am PDT** | Live Q&A. |
| **27 Oct – 3 Nov 2026** | Early name reservation + character creation. Up to **3 characters**. |
| **4 Nov 2026, 3:00 pm PST** | **Launch** = **10:00 am Thu 5 Nov AEDT** (Sydney). |
| **9 Dec 2026** | **All three raids unlock** — Barrow Deeps (10), Hyjal Summit (20), Onyxia's Lair (40). |
| **Winter 2026/27** | **Hardcore** — a **ruleset**, not separate realms (**corrected 14 Sep**, see below). "Shortly after release." No date; **not in the initial beta build**. |
| **Spring 2027** | 2 new raids (one 10-player, one 20-player), 2 new dungeons, new quests, new playable area, new legendary questline, PvP season refresh. |
| **Summer 2027** | A revamped iconic raid (**unnamed**), a new raid, expanded world content, 2 new dungeons, PvP season refresh, professions + Legacy updates. |

Blizzard's own caveat: exact release windows and content are **subject to change**.

## Sydney conversions

[SPECULATION] These are calculated, not published by Blizzard.

| Event | Source time | Sydney |
|---|---|---|
| Deep Dive panel | Sun 13 Sep, 10:00 am PDT | **03:00 Mon 14 Sep AEST** |
| Live Q&A | Thu 17 Sep, 10:00 am PDT | **03:00 Fri 18 Sep AEST** |
| Launch | Wed 4 Nov, 3:00 pm **PST** | **10:00 Thu 5 Nov AEDT** |

**DST mechanics that make these easy to get wrong:** the US leaves daylight time on **1 Nov**
and Australia enters AEDT on **4 Oct**. So launch is genuinely PST (not PDT), and September
events convert to **AEST** — not AEDT.

> [!warning] An official Blizzard article contradicts itself on this — the table above is right
> **[Tier 1, both sides]** Article **24302093** *("Carve a New Path with World of Warcraft:
> Forever")* gives the launch as *"November 4 at 3:00 p.m. **PDT** globally"* in one place and
> *"November 4, 2026, at 3:00 p.m. **PST**"* in another, **within the same article**. The
> pre-purchase article (**24301508**) says **PST**.
>
> **PST is correct** — US daylight time ends 1 November 2026, so 4 November cannot be PDT.
> **[14 Sep, second check]** The **Collector's Edition** article (24302498) also says
> *"November 4, 2026, at 3:00 p.m. PST"*, making it **three official PST mentions against one
> PDT** — and the lone PDT is a second sentence inside an article whose other sentence says PST.
> **This table stands.**
>
> The stakes are exactly one hour in Sydney: 3pm PST = **10:00 Thu 5 Nov AEDT**, whereas 3pm PDT
> would be 09:00. Flagged here so nobody re-derives the conversion from the wrong sentence.
> See [[Post-Panel Interviews 2026-09-14]].

> [!warning] Correction — September conversions were mislabelled AEDT
> An earlier note gave the Deep Dive panel as "3:00am Mon 14 Sep **AEDT**". The clock time is
> right; the zone label is wrong. Sydney is on **AEST** until 4 October. The note's own DST
> line contradicted its conversion.

> likely both used "realm" loosely before the structure was explained. **No outlet has published a

> favour of the later, official, more specific source.
>
> Consequence for [[Open Questions]]: *"Hardcore realm count"* is **moot, not answered** — there
> is no such number to find. The **ruleset** half of that question is now answered. Full detail in
> [[Realms and Rulesets]].
>
> **[Tier 2]** Output Lag adds that Hardcore is **not in the beta build** on 17 Sep and that **no
> date was given** at the Hardcore panel. The Winter 2026/27 season label from the roadmap slide is

**Beta end date — conflict, official wins.** Blizzard's pre-purchase article and Warcraft
Tavern both state the beta runs **17 Sep – 21 Oct 2026**. Icy Veins' and Blizzard Watch's
roadmap write-ups both read the slide as **17 Sep – 22 Oct**. **[14 Sep]** Blizzard Watch's
Deep-Dive-day roadmap piece still says **22 Oct**, so the outlet reading has not shifted.
**[14 Sep, second check]** Blizzard's **Collector's Edition** article (**24302498**) independently
states beta access runs *"September 17 through October 21 PDT"* — a **third** official mention of
**21 October**, from a different article than the two already counted. The official side of this
conflict is now 3–0; the outlet side is unchanged at 2. **21 October stands, more firmly.** Per the source hierarchy the
official date holds: **21 October**. Flagging because two independent outlets read 22, so the
slide itself may say 22 and the article may be the one that is off by a day. Low stakes,
worth a glance at beta's end.

## Regional rollout — updates will not land everywhere at once

**[Tier 2]** In a post-BlizzCon interview, **Ion Hazzikostas** described *"staggered rollouts of
our updates… across regions"*, reasoning that a globally simultaneous patch would put European
servers *"offline from like 5 p.m. to 11 p.m."*

**This matters for Sydney.** Every conversion on this page is derived from a **US** source time.
If content unlocks are staggered by region, the APAC unlock for a post-launch patch is a separate
scheduling decision and **may not track these conversions at all**. The 4 November **launch** was
explicitly stated as global; the staggering claim is about **updates**. Detail in
[[Post-Panel Interviews 2026-09-14]].

## Gaps

- **Nothing is scheduled between 9 Dec 2026 and spring 2027** — a 3+ month content gap.
- No patch cadence published at all.
- No specific dates for the spring or summer 2027 windows.
- Hardcore's placement is a season label on the roadmap, not a date — **re-confirmed 14 Sep**,
  the Hardcore panel gave none.
- **[Tier 2]** Blizzard Watch's roadmap write-up notes the 2027 windows are *"subject to change and
  can always be moved up or back, or removed outright"*, which matches Blizzard's own caveat.
