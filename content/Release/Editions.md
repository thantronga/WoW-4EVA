# Editions — WoW Forever

[CONFIRMED] **A WoW subscription or Game Time is required on top of any pack.** The packs are
upgrades, not standalone purchases.

## The beta trap

Worth being explicit because it catches people: **the \$29.99 Heroic Pack does not include
beta access.** Blizzard's own pre-purchase page states "Does not include beta access". Beta is
**Epic (\$59.99) and above only**.

So if the goal is beta on 17 Sep, the entry price is **\$59.99**, not \$29.99.

## The Collector's Edition — closed 14 September 2026

> [!success] Both previously-unverified figures are now confirmed
> Blizzard published a **dedicated Collector's Edition article** (**24302498**) and the **Blizzard
> Gear Store listing is live**. The vault's two carried-forward [REPORTED] figures were both right.

**[Tier 1 — Gear Store listing]**

- **Price: \$150.00 USD.**
- **Ship date:** *"This is a pre-order product and is not available to ship until November 2026."*
- **Status right now: Sold Out** on the US Gear Store.
- **Shipping restriction:** *"Orders ship only to select locations in North and South America."*

**Contents.** Physical: a **13-inch Dwarf and Bear statue**, a **Zephras Isle mousepad**, **art
prints**, **collectible pins** (compass and Skyborne crest), and a **companion journal**. Digital:
the **Warcraft Forever Collection code** — which is the \$79.99 tier, so it carries the Skyborne
Epic Pack, **beta access (17 Sep – 21 Oct)**, **30 days Game Time**, *Warcraft III: Reforged* and
the *Forsaken Kingdom* campaign, plus cosmetics, mounts, transmog, tabards, pets and housing decor,
and Invite-A-Friend codes.

> [!important] For Australia — the US Gear Store will not sell you this
> Blizzard's own article: *"Players in Australia and New Zealand can pre-purchase through
> **participating regional retailers**. Check your local retailer for availability."* The Gear
> Store's North-and-South-America-only shipping is why. There is a **separate EU Gear Store
> listing** (~€150), but commenters there report **€200–€300+ international shipping**, so it is
> not a sensible route from Sydney either.
>
> **[Tier 4 — community report, unverified]** A Blizzard forum thread titled *"Collector's
> Edition: Unavailable for Australians"* has an Australian player blocked at checkout, and
> **other posters naming EB Games and JB Hi-Fi**, with one saying *"It's up on EB Games now,
> behind their subscription however"* and another reporting a successful pre-order the next day.
> **There is no blue reply, and no EB Games or JB Hi-Fi listing could be verified directly.**
> Treat as a lead to check yourself, not as fact.

**Note the beta implication.** The CE includes the Collection code, so it **does** include beta
access — unlike the \$29.99 Heroic Pack. But it is **sold out** on the only store that lists a
price, so it is not a route to beta by 17 September.
