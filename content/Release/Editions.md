# Editions — WoW Forever

[CONFIRMED] **A WoW subscription or Game Time is required on top of any pack.** The packs are
upgrades, not standalone purchases.

---

| Edition | Price | Includes |
|---|---|---|
| **Skyborne Heroic Pack** | **\$29.99** | Skyborne race + Zephras Isle starting experience, early name reservation, *Cerulean Prideclaw* ground mount, *Shen'dorei Skyseer's Garb* transmog, *Veteran Adventurer's Rucksack*, *Shen'dorei Windwell* toy, Skyborne housing decor, 1× Invite-A-Friend code. **Does NOT include beta access.** |
| **Skyborne Epic Pack** | **\$59.99** | Everything above, **plus beta access** (17 Sep – 21 Oct 2026), **30 days Game Time** (starts 4 Nov), an additional mount and transmog set, pets, tabards, **3× Invite-A-Friend codes**. |
| **Warcraft Forever Collection** | **\$79.99** | Everything above, plus **Warcraft III: Reforged** and the new **Forsaken Kingdom** campaign, plus figurine housing decor for modern WoW. **Limited availability through 11 Jan 2027.** |
| **Physical Collector's Edition** | [REPORTED] **\$150** | Ships **November 2026**. Via the Blizzard Gear Store. |

---

## The beta trap

Worth being explicit because it catches people: **the \$29.99 Heroic Pack does not include
beta access.** Blizzard's own pre-purchase page states "Does not include beta access". Beta is
**Epic (\$59.99) and above only**.

So if the goal is beta on 17 Sep, the entry price is **\$59.99**, not \$29.99.

## Verification notes, 13 Sep 2026

**Confirmed:** all three digital prices (\$29.99 / \$59.99 / \$79.99), the Collection's 11 Jan
2027 cut-off, and the Heroic-excludes-beta point. Prices are absent from Blizzard's own news
articles and were confirmed via Warcraft Tavern, Wowhead, MMORPG.com and the Battle.net shop
listing; Icy Veins independently gives \$29.99/€29.99 for the race unlock.

**Not verified:** the **\$150** Collector's Edition price and the **November 2026** ship date.
Blizzard's pre-purchase article references a physical Collector's Edition but gives neither
figure, and none of the coverage checked carried them. Both are carried forward as
[REPORTED] — **check the Gear Store listing directly before relying on either.**

**Note the paid-race criticism** — the \$30 race gate is the single biggest source of community
pushback on the announcement. See [[Skyborne]].
