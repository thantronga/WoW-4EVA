# Skyborne — WoW Forever

> [!info] Racials, the full race/class matrix and the demo-read trait text live in
> [[Races, Racials and Combos]]. This note covers identity, lore, faction split and the
> paywall. Deliberately not duplicated.

---

## The basics

[CONFIRMED] A new playable race. **Faction is chosen at character creation**, Pandaren-style.
Shared ancestry, now divergent visions.

| | Exclusive class | Shared classes | Demo label |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Alliance** — Kirin Tor ties | **Mage** | Warrior, Hunter, Rogue, Druid | High Order Skyborne |
| **Horde** — Earthen Ring ties | **Shaman** | Warrior, Hunter, Rogue, Druid | Windshaper Skyborne |

**No access to Paladin, Priest or Warlock** on either side.

New racials, customisations, voices, dances and visual options.

> [!note] Massively OP has the faction/class split reversed
> It states Alliance = Shaman, Horde = Mage. The official Blizzard recap and three other
> outlets say the opposite. **Horde = Shaman, Alliance = Mage.**

## Lore origin — upgraded from speculation

> [!important] Correction 13 Sep 2026 — this was under-rated as "fan site only"
> The Highborne/Skywall origin was previously tagged **[SPECULATION — fan site only]** on the
> grounds that Blizzard's own text says only "shared ancestry + diverging visions". That
> under-rates it. The evidence is now:
>
> 1. **Blizzard's own pre-purchase copy** names the Skyborne cosmetics **"Shen'dorei"** —
>    *Shen'dorei Skyseer's Garb*, *Shen'dorei Windwell*. `-dorei` is the Darnassian/Thalassian
>    "children of" construction (quel'dorei, kal'dorei, sin'dorei). That is an official-source
>    elven marker, not a fan inference.
> 2. The **demo build** showed the in-fiction term **"shen'dorei"** in-world, alongside
>    Thendal Village / Thendal Grove and *Rorian the Dayseeker*.
> 3. Multiple outlets (wccftech among them) state the origin outright: **Highborne exiles who
>    pacted with the wind elementals of Skywall**, building a Skywall-inspired civilisation.
>
> **Revised tier: Tier 2** (multiple independent outlets + official naming corroboration),
> not Tier 4 inference. Still short of Tier 1 — Blizzard has not narrated the origin on
> stage, and Icy Veins notes Blizzard has deliberately **withheld** detailed lore, referring
> only to "the mysterious sky kingdom they inhabit".
>
> Note that this vault's own [[Races, Racials and Combos]] note already had this as
> **Confirmed**. The brief's downgrade contradicted an existing note — reconciled here in
> favour of Tier 2.

[SPECULATION] The **"Shen'dorei" / "Shen'dralas" / "Shen'dralar"** name cluster is unlikely to
be coincidence. Shen'dralas sits between Mulgore and Desolace and ties to the Shen'dralar
highborne of Eldre'Thalas. If the Skyborne are Highborne-descended, the new mid-level zone and
the new race may be halves of one story. **Not stated by Blizzard.** Worth watching in beta.

## Druid forms

[REPORTED] **Race-specific druid forms** — bear, cat, travel and moonkin, in **sky-blue**, not
shared with night elf or tauren. Blizzard confirmed forms built specifically for the race;
the colour and the specific four are third-party observation.

**No tree form shown.** Restoration form remains unrevealed — still open.

> [!important] Still open after the Deep Dive panel — 14 Sep 2026, and the "moonkin" claim is weaker than it looked
> The Deep Dive panel said nothing about Skyborne druid forms. Wowhead's dedicated article on
> the subject shows **three** forms and three only:
>
> - **Bear Form** — "a hybrid of a bird and a bear"
> - **Cat Form** — "a hybrid of a bird and a cat, very similar to the Owlcats"
> - **Travel Form** — a bird form, notable because **there is no flying in Forever**
>
> It does **not** mention moonkin, aquatic, flight or Tree of Life. This note previously listed
> **moonkin** among the four observed forms; that now rests on a single earlier third-party
> observation rather than a cluster. **Downgrade moonkin to single-source** until beta.
>
> **Restoration / Tree of Life remains unrevealed by anyone.** Still open. See
> [[Deep Dive Panel 2026-09-14]].

## Racials

Now known — see [[Races, Racials and Combos]] for the full text. Summary:

- **Both variants:** *Walk on Air* (glide downward for 10 sec), *Wind Blessed* (P, +1% haste),
  *Elemental Insight* (P, +5% damage to Elementals)
- **Alliance only:** *Read Ley Line*
- **Horde only:** *Skysight*

> [!important] This closes a previously open question
> "Skyborne racial abilities — not revealed" was listed as open. It is not: the racials were
> read off the BlizzCon demo build's character-creation panels on 12 Sep and are already
> recorded in this vault. They are **Tier 2/3** (demo-read, not announced on stage), so the
> residual open item is *official confirmation*, not the ability names.

## The paywall

[CONFIRMED] Race **and** starting zone are locked behind the **Skyborne Heroic Pack (\$29.99 /
€29.99)** or higher. A WoW subscription is required on top. See [[Editions]].

This is drawing the heaviest community pushback of the announcement — "why not High
Elves/Ogres" and "more elves" are both running jokes, and the \$30 race gate for a
Classic+ product got its own critical coverage (MMORPG.com, Blizzard forum threads).
