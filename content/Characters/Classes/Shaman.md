# Shaman — WoW Forever

## The one-line version

Lava Burst, Maelstrom Weapon, Water Shield and Riptide all arrive; Stormstrike moves down the
tree and becomes an instant strike; the Elemental tree is renamed **Elemental Combat**.

## What Blizzard actually said

Nothing Shaman-specific — except that **Dwarves can now be Shaman** (Alliance) and
**Horde-aligned Skyborne** get Shaman as their faction-exclusive class.

The "Shaman gets a new spec" claim circulating on MMO-Champion is from a **fabricated leak
thread** that the community debunked. There is no evidence for it. See
[[Sources and Confidence]].

## What the data shows

### Tree renamed

The first tree is labelled **Elemental Combat** in the demo build, not Elemental. Read from
the footage with high confidence.

### New baseline abilities (from the demo spellbook)

**Call of the Ancestors**, **Call of the Elements**, **Totemic Recall** and **Fire Nova** as a
*spell* (not just a totem) all appear on a level-38 Shaman's Elemental Combat page. Totem-set
management from TBC has been backported.

### Elemental Combat

- **Lava Burst** — new capstone. Bonus damage if Flame Shock is on the target.
- **Lightning Overload** — Lightning Bolt and Chain Lightning can fire a free second cast.
- **Elemental Alacrity** — flat cast-time reduction on the core nukes.
- **Elemental Reach**, **Improved Fire Nova**, **Earthbound** (Earthbind Totem immobilises).
- *Elemental Fury* went from **1 rank to 5**; *Call of Thunder* went from **5 ranks to 1**.
- *Eye of the Storm* was rewritten from a Focused-Casting proc into flat pushback reduction.

### Enhancement

- **Maelstrom Weapon** — melee hits reduce the cast time and mana cost of your next Lightning
  Bolt, stacking to 5. The single most important import in the tree.
- **Improved Stormstrike** — mana regen proc plus a cooldown reset on dodge/parry.
- **Mental Quickness** and **Mental Dexterity** — Intellect converts into spell power *and*
  attack power.
- **Shamanistic Focus**, **Spirit Weapons** (threat control), **Rage of the Farseer** (new
  capstone: +30% melee and cast speed).
- *Stormstrike* moved from the capstone slot to row 4 and was rewritten from "gives you an
  extra attack" into an **instant strike for normal weapon damage** plus a Nature damage
  debuff.
- *Thundering Strikes* now increases crit with **all spells and attacks**, not just weapons.
- *Toughness* changed from armour to **Stamina**; *Ancestral Knowledge* from maximum mana to
  **Intellect**.

### Restoration

- **Water Shield** and **Riptide** (capstone) both arrive.
- **Mindfulness** (mana regen while casting) and **Natural Grace** (threat reduction).
- *Mana Tide Totem* moved off the capstone slot down to row 4.
- *Healing Way* was rewritten from a stacking Healing Wave buff into a flat increase — simpler,
  and removes the ramp.
- *Tidal Focus* now also grants hit chance.

### What fell out

*Elemental Mastery*, *Lightning Mastery*, *Concussion*, *Totemic Mastery*, *Nature's Guidance*,
*Two-Handed Axes and Maces*, *Weapon Mastery*, *Parry* and *Shield Specialization* are absent
from the read data. Two-Handed Axes and Maces becoming baseline is plausible but unconfirmed.

## My read (inference, not fact)

Maelstrom Weapon plus Mental Quickness plus Mental Dexterity means Enhancement is being built
as a genuine hybrid that gains from Intellect — an entirely different gearing model to Classic
Enhancement. Elemental losing *Elemental Mastery* while gaining *Lightning Overload* swaps a
burst cooldown for sustained throughput, which is a sensible raid-design choice.

## Data gaps

Three cells in Elemental Combat were never hovered (13 of 16 read).
