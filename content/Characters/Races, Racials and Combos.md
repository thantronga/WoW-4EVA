# Races, Racials and Combos — WoW Forever

Blizzard confirmed that racial abilities were reworked to feel **"more unique than before"**,
and that racial choice should matter more at character creation. The detail below is read from
the demo build's character-creation screens, cross-checked against a second independent
write-up that agrees on essentially every line.

---

## The Skyborne

**Confirmed:** a new playable race, the **Skyborne** — elves descended from Highborne exiles
who made a pact with the wind elementals of Skywall generations ago. Their starting zone is
**Zephras Isle**, levels 1–12, a floating island. In-world the demo showed zone names
**Thendal Village** and **Thendal Grove**, a quest *Coming of Age* from **Rorian the Dayseeker**,
and the in-fiction term **"shen'dorei"**.

**They pick a faction at character creation**, and the faction determines one exclusive class.
The demo labelled the two variants **High Order Skyborne** (Alliance) and **Windshaper
Skyborne** (Horde).

| | Exclusive class | Shared classes |
|---|---|---|
| **Alliance — High Order** | **Mage** | Warrior, Hunter, Rogue, Druid |
| **Horde — Windshaper** | **Shaman** | Warrior, Hunter, Rogue, Druid |

Skyborne **cannot be Paladins** on either side. Blizzard confirmed the Skyborne get **new Druid
forms created specifically for the race**, plus their own racials, customisations, voices and
dances.

> [!note] One source has this backwards
> Massively OP's deep-dive write-up states Alliance gains Shaman and Horde gains Mage. Three
> other sources including the official Blizzard panel recap say the opposite. Go with
> **Horde = Shaman, Alliance = Mage**.

**Access is paid.** The Skyborne race unlock requires the **Skyborne Heroic Pack ($29.99)** or
a higher edition. This drew immediate criticism.

## Full race/class matrix

Bold = new in Forever. Read off the lit/greyed class bar in the demo's character creation
screen, and corroborated by two independent outlets.

| Race | Classes |
|---|---|
| **Human** | Warrior, **Hunter**, Mage, Rogue, Priest, Warlock, Paladin |
| **Dwarf** | Warrior, Hunter, Rogue, Priest, Paladin, **Shaman** |
| **Night Elf** | Warrior, Hunter, Rogue, Priest, Druid |
| **Gnome** | Warrior, Mage, Rogue, **Priest**, Warlock |
| **Skyborne (Alliance)** | **Warrior, Hunter, Mage, Rogue, Druid** |
| **Orc** | Warrior, Hunter, **Mage**, Rogue, Warlock, Shaman |
| **Troll** | Warrior, Hunter, Mage, Rogue, Priest, **Warlock**, Shaman |
| **Tauren** | Warrior, Hunter, Druid, Shaman |
| **Undead** | Warrior, Mage, Rogue, Priest, Warlock, **Paladin** |
| **Skyborne (Horde)** | **Warrior, Hunter, Rogue, Druid, Shaman** |

So: **eight new core combinations** — Human Hunter, Dwarf Shaman, Gnome Priest, Orc Mage, Troll
Warlock, Undead Paladin — plus ten Skyborne combinations.

**Undead Paladin is the one to flag.** It was **not** observed on screen: the Undead race panel
never appeared in the demo footage. It is reported by multiple outlets, and Blizzard reportedly
revealed it via a **new cinematic**. Confident, but sourced differently from the rest.

Combos *not* confirmed either way: Tauren Priest, Tauren Paladin, Dwarf Mage, Night Elf Paladin.
One Wowhead summary mentions **Night Elf Paladin**; no other source does and the demo class bar
does not show it. Treat as unconfirmed.

## Racial abilities

Read from character creation panels. Roughly **60% of racial traits are new or reworked**.
Passives marked *(P)*.

### Alliance

**Human**
- *Will to Survive* — removes Stuns. **New** (replaces Classic's Perception-era kit shape)
- *Perception* — detect stealthed enemies for 20 sec. Reworded
- *Sword Specialization (P)* — swords increase **spell and ability critical strike chance by
  2%**. **Reworked** — weapon specs now give crit, not weapon skill
- *The Human Spirit (P)* — +5% Spirit
- *(Mace Specialization and Diplomacy were not visible on screen)*

**Dwarf**
- *Stoneform* — immunity to Bleeds, Poisons and Diseases **and** reduced physical damage taken
  for 8 sec. **Reworked** (Classic gave +10% armour)
- *Find Treasure* — tracks nearby treasure chests
- *Mace Specialization (P)* — maces increase spell and ability crit by 1%. **Reworked**
- *Big Game Hunter (P)* — +5% damage to Beasts

**Night Elf**
- *Elune's Light* — **+10% critical strike chance for 15 sec. New active racial**
- *Shadowmeld* — stealth while stationary
- *Quickness (P)* — +1% dodge **and +2% run speed**. **Reworked**
- *Wisp Spirit (P)* — +75% run speed while dead

**Gnome**
- *Escape Artist* — brief immunity to Roots and Snares. Reworked
- *Eureka!* — **New**: reduces the cost of your next three spells or abilities and increases
  their damage or healing by 10%
- *Expansive Mind (P)* — +5% maximum **Mana, Rage or Energy**. **Reworked** — now covers all
  resources, so it is finally useful to Warriors and Rogues
- *Engineering Specialization (P)* — more reliable engineering devices

**Skyborne (Alliance / High Order)** — all new
- *Walk on Air* — glide downward through the air for 10 sec
- *Read Ley Line* — activate a ley line for +100% health and mana regeneration
- *Wind Blessed (P)* — +1% melee, ranged and spellcasting haste
- *Elemental Insight (P)* — +5% damage to Elementals

### Horde

**Orc**
- *Blood Fury* — **+10% attack power and spell power** for 15 sec. **Reworked** — now
  percentage-based and buffs casters
- *Shatter Curse* — **New**: immunity to Curses and Banes, plus reduced magical damage taken
  for 8 sec
- *Axe Specialization (P)* — axes increase spell and ability crit by 1%. **Reworked**
- *Hardiness (P)* — stun duration reduced by 20% (Classic: 25%)
- *Command* appears to be **gone**

**Undead**
- *Will of the Forsaken* — removes charm, fear and sleep
- *Cannibalize* — consume corpses for 35% health **and mana**. Reworked
- *Underwater Breathing (P)* — +300% underwater breath
- *Touch of the Grave (P)* — **New**: your attacks sometimes drain health. A later-expansion
  racial backported

**Tauren**
- *War Stomp* — stuns nearby enemies for 2 sec
- *Cultivation* — grow bonus herbs gatherable without Herbalism. **Reworked**
- *Plainsrunning (P)* — **New**: movement speed increases the longer you keep moving. This is
  the famous cut alpha racial, restored
- *Endurance (P)* — +5% total health **and +1% hit chance**. **Reworked**
- *(Nature Resistance not seen)*

**Troll**
- *Berserking* — +10% casting and attack speed for 10 sec
- *Rapid Regeneration* — regenerate 50% of maximum health over time
- *Beast Slaying (P)* — +5% damage to Beasts
- *Regeneration (P)* — 10% of health regeneration continues in combat

**Skyborne (Horde / Windshaper)** — all new
- *Walk on Air* — shared with the Alliance variant
- *Skysight* — an Elemental Blessing granting +10% run speed. The variant-specific active
- *Wind Blessed (P)* — shared
- *Elemental Insight (P)* — shared

## Why this matters for class play

Three racials are now genuinely build-relevant rather than flavour:

1. **Weapon specialisations give crit instead of weapon skill** across Human, Dwarf and Orc.
   Weapon skill was a hidden, badly-explained Classic stat; crit is legible and stacks with
   everything. This makes Human/Orc/Dwarf melee meaningfully stronger and makes the choice
   readable at character creation, which is exactly the stated goal.
2. **Gnome *Expansive Mind* now covers Rage and Energy**, so Gnome Warrior and Gnome Rogue gain
   a real resource benefit for the first time.
3. **Night Elf *Elune's Light*** is a 10% crit cooldown on a race previously chosen for dodge
   and Shadowmeld. That is a DPS racial on the Alliance's Druid/Hunter/Rogue race.

**Data caveat:** the Undead race panel was never shown, so Undead trait *text* is inferred from
the spellbook's General page listing rather than read from a tooltip. Troll's *Berserking* and
Dwarf's remaining traits scrolled off screen in the frames available.

## Verification note — 13 September 2026

**The Highborne/Skywall origin is better sourced than "demo build + one write-up".** Blizzard's
own pre-purchase copy names the Skyborne cosmetics **"Shen'dorei"** — *Shen'dorei Skyseer's
Garb*, *Shen'dorei Windwell*. `-dorei` is the Darnassian/Thalassian "children of" construction
(kal'dorei, quel'dorei, sin'dorei), so this is an **official-source elven marker**, independent
of the demo and of any fan site. Multiple outlets also state the Highborne-exiles-and-Skywall
origin outright.

This note's "Confirmed" framing therefore stands. A later research pass tried to downgrade it
to "[SPECULATION — fan site only]" on the grounds that Blizzard's stage text says only "shared
ancestry"; that under-rated it. **Tier 2, not Tier 4.** Blizzard has still not narrated the
origin on stage — Icy Veins notes the lore is deliberately withheld, referring only to "the
mysterious sky kingdom they inhabit" — so it is not Tier 1 either.

**The Skyborne racials recorded above were also independently confirmed** against a second
demo-build write-up (Warcraft Tavern, 12 Sep): *Walk on Air*, *Wind Blessed*, *Elemental
Insight* on both variants, *Read Ley Line* on Alliance, *Skysight* on Horde. A later pass
listed these as "not revealed" — they were already here, and correct.

**The two on-stage racial examples check out** against this note's demo-read list: Dwarf
*Big Game Hunter* (+5% damage to Beasts) is the "bonus damage against beasts" example, and
Undead *Touch of the Grave* is the "chance for spells to drain health" example. Note Blizzard
said *spells*; the demo tooltip says *attacks*. Minor, unresolved.

[SPECULATION] The **Shen'dorei / Shen'dralas / Shen'dralar** name cluster — a new mid-level
zone between Mulgore and Desolace tied to the Shen'dralar highborne, and a new highborne-
descended race — is unlikely to be coincidence. Not stated by Blizzard. See [[Skyborne]].
