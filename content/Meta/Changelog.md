- **2026-09-14 (post-panel research pass)** — **Interviews, show-floor hands-on, and a re-read of
  the official articles.** New note [[Post-Panel Interviews 2026-09-14]]. **Biggest find:**
  Blizzard's own article 24302093 states the timeline placement — *"after the events of Warcraft
  III Reforged: Forsaken Kingdom and before Molten Core"* — closing a residual this vault carried
  as a low-authority wiki placement marked "do not quote as fact". **Third time in three days**
  an answer was already sitting in an officially-cited source. **New from interviews (Tier 2,
  single-outlet):** the new raids need **no attunement at launch**; **raid sizes are fixed, no
  flex scaling**; updates will use **staggered regional rollouts**; Season of Discovery is now
  described as having *overshot* rather than merely mapped the limit. **New from hands-on
  (Tier 3):** two-part names were visible on the floor a day before the realmless reveal;
  **Edit Mode** UI customisation; only **meta-level Legacy challenges award points**; the full
  gamepad binding scheme. **Source change:** Wowhead's Forever talent calculator went live with
  **real data** on 13 Sep — the *DO NOT FILE* placeholder warning is **retired, not reversed**,
  replaced with "Tier 2 for names, unusable for ranks". **New conflicts:** an official Blizzard
  article gives the launch as both **PST and PDT** (PST is correct); the Legacy Reward Track
  triggers on **overflow** (official) vs **points spent** (hands-on); Riverglades gains a
  **fourth** level reading (30–45). Merged into [[Raids]], [[Zones]], [[Dungeons]], [[Roadmap]],
  [[Systems]], [[Talent System]], [[Design Pillars]], [[Realms and Rulesets]],
  [[Open Questions]], [[Sources and Confidence]] and [[Deep Dive Panel 2026-09-14]].

- **2026-09-14 (world buffs check)** — **Answered a direct question with a negative finding, and
  filed it.** **Blizzard has said nothing about world buffs in Forever** — verified across both
  official recaps, the pre-purchase, "Carve a New Path" and panel-schedule articles, all three
  post-BlizzCon interviews, every hands-on write-up, the Hardcore panel coverage, PC Gamer's and
  Inven Global's round-ups, and the Forever forum. New section
  [[Systems|Systems § World buffs and the buff economy]] records the absence plus the four
  adjacent signals that lean toward *less* stacking (campsite buffs not stacking with class buffs;
  party-buff talents going baseline; raid debuffs de-stacked; the "un-solve solved builds"
  framing), and notes that **Onyxia's Lair is in the game on 9 Dec** — so the source of *Rallying
  Cry of the Dragonslayer* exists — while **Blackwing Lair is not announced**, so *Warchief's
  Blessing* has no source. Cross-noted in [[Raids]] and tracked in [[Open Questions]].
  **Also found while checking:** Blizzard's schedule article gives the Sunday panel slate, which
  exposes a **naming trap** — the panel this vault calls "the Deep Dive" is the 10:00 am
  *"Forever: Evolving a Timeless World"* slot, while a **separate** *"WoW: Design Deep Dive"* ran
  at 2:15 pm. Recorded in [[Sources and Confidence]] with the Housing/World Building/Art Design
  panels flagged as **not** Forever-marked. **A third Lead Software Engineer (Jason Scott)** turns
  up in that coverage, which tips the **Nora Mills vs Ana Resendez** title collision toward
  "several people hold the title" — see [[Design Pillars]]. The graphics-panel content and its two
  presenter names are **MMO-Champion single-source with a slot label contradicting Blizzard's own
  recap**, so they are recorded and **not adopted**. World-buff Classic/Anniversary guides added to
  the contamination register.
