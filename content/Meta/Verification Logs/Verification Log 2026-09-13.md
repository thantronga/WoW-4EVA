## Corrections

### 1. Krol'dok Stronghold — 40–55, in the Riverglades ✅ *(was 35–40, zone unknown)*
Six sources give **40–55**: Icy Veins, Output Lag, Out of Games, wccftech, wowtbc.gg,
goldgoblin. Only MMO-Champion gives 35–40 — the same transcription that omitted the Lordaeron
and Dalaran brackets. It is an **open-world/outdoor dungeon**, which explains the 15-level
spread. The standing MODERATE-confidence theory that it sits inside Riverglades was **correct**.

### 2. Blackmaw Hold — Azshara ❌ *(HIGH-confidence Felwood/Winterspring theory disproven)*
**Blackmaw Hold is a pre-existing furbolg location in Azshara**, with its own wiki entry and a
Cataclysm-era map position. Three outlets place the Forever dungeon there. This is a hard
disconfirmation, not a vote count. The single most confident inference in the research was the
only one that failed.

### 3. The Drowned City — Stranglethorn coast ✅, but **troll** ruins ❌
Location pick correct. The Zin-Azshari / Highborne lore hypothesis is **superseded**: Mills
described *"sunken troll ruins that have suddenly risen out of the ocean"* — trolls, naga and
a crashed pirate ship. One outlet (goldgoblin) says Hinterlands coast instead; two say
Stranglethorn, and the Mills quote carries Stranglethorn.

### 4. Ruins of Lordaeron 15–20 and City of Dalaran 28–33 are now firm
Both previously soft or missing. Lordaeron 15–20: three sources. Dalaran 28–33: four sources;
Output Lag's 28–30 is the outlier.

### 5. Skyborne racials were **already known** — the open question was stale
*Walk on Air*, *Wind Blessed*, *Elemental Insight*, plus *Read Ley Line* (Alliance) /
*Skysight* (Horde). Read off the demo build on 12 Sep and **already written into this vault's
own [[Races, Racials and Combos]] note.** The open-questions list contradicted an existing
note.

### 6. Skyborne lore origin was under-rated as "fan site only"
Blizzard's own pre-purchase copy names the cosmetics **"Shen'dorei"** — an official elven
naming marker. Combined with the in-demo term and multiple outlets stating the Highborne/
Skywall origin outright, this is **Tier 2**, not Tier 4 inference. Again, this vault's existing
note already had it as Confirmed.

## Method note

The failure pattern worth keeping: **two of the three location theories were right, and the one
that failed was the one held with highest confidence.** The MODERATE and LOW calls reasoned
from things Blizzard had said in the same panel. The HIGH call reasoned from lore themes
(corrupted furbolgs, tunnel networks, naming symmetry) toward a *new* location — without
checking whether "Blackmaw Hold" was already a place on the Azshara map. It was.

**Rule going forward: before theorising a location for a named place, search the name against
existing Azeroth geography first.** Three of the nine dungeon names (Blackmaw Hold, Alcaz
Island, Dalaran) are pre-existing locations.
