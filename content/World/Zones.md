# Zones — WoW Forever

[CONFIRMED] Scope is **Eastern Kingdoms + Kalimdor only**. **1,000+ new quests** across the
full 1–60 range.

Marketing says **"three new zones"**; the panel showed **four regions**. Shen'dralas is the
most likely one being classed as a reimagining rather than a new zone — Warcraft Tavern reads
it that way too, since it extends existing Dire Maul / Maraudon / centaur lore rather than
introducing new geography.

---

## The four

### Mount Hyjal (Renewed)
**Endgame zone.** The aftermath of Archimonde's defeat. Includes **Darkwhisper Gorge**, which
is also the access route. Solo *and* group content, multiple questlines, new factions and
reputations to grind, new rewards.

Hosts **both** new raids: [[Raids|Hyjal Summit]] and one of the three **Barrow Deeps**
entrances.

### Shen'dralas
Between **Mulgore and Desolace**. Ties to the **Shen'dralar**, **Eldre'Thalas**, **Dire Maul**
and the regional **centaur** tribes. New reasons to enter **Maraudon**.
[REPORTED] Also connected to **Razorfen Downs** narratives. Level range not stated by
Blizzard; Warcraft Tavern estimates **~30–50**.

Worth noting the name proximity to **"Shen'dorei"**, the in-fiction term for the
[[Skyborne]] — see that note.

### The Riverglades
**Eastern Kingdoms.** Rivers, grasslands, trade routes, struggling settlements, old grudges —
a rugged frontier. **150+ new quests**, the largest new zone. A **neutral goblin trading
company** / port. A **powerful ogre civilisation**, which is the
[[Dungeons|Krol'dok Stronghold]] dungeon.

[REPORTED] Geography: east coast of the Eastern Kingdoms, bordered by **Redridge Mountains,
Swamp of Sorrows and the Badlands**.

> [!note] Level range conflict

> supports. But wccftech reads the slide as **35–55**, and Warcraft Tavern as **~40–55**.
> The Krol'dok Stronghold dungeon inside this zone is **40–55**, which pulls toward the wider
> reading — a dungeon is not usually 10 levels above its host zone's ceiling. **Unresolved;
> lean wider than mid-40s.**
>
> **Fourth reading added 14 Sep.** Blizzard Watch's zones/dungeons/raids round-up (13 Sep) gives
> Riverglades as **30–45**. That is the **lowest ceiling anyone has proposed**, and it sits worst
> of the four with the 40–55 dungeon inside the zone. Recorded for completeness, **not adopted**.
>
> Tally of readings now in circulation: **mid-30s–mid-40s** (Output Lag) · **30–45** (Blizzard
> Watch) · **35–55** (wccftech) · **~40–55** (Warcraft Tavern). Four outlets, four answers, from
> one slide. This is the clearest single illustration of why every level figure in this vault is
> Tier 2. Beta on 17 Sep settles it.

### Zephras Isle
**Skyborne starting zone, levels 1–12.** A floating/skyborne island, elementally active, with
a civilisation in crisis (themes of corruption and contested power). Classic aesthetics
combined with **Skywall-inspired** architecture.

[REPORTED] Demo showed in-world locations **Thendal Village** and **Thendal Grove**, and a
starting quest *Coming of Age* from **Rorian the Dayseeker**.

**Paywalled** — access requires the Skyborne Heroic Pack or higher. See [[Editions]].

## Also reworked

**Dalaran** relocates to / is playable in the **Alterac Mountains** as an interactive city
with its own dungeon and questlines, not just a dungeon entrance. See [[Dungeons]].

General world rendering updates across existing zones — flowing rivers in Elwynn, mist over
Darkshore water, moonlight through Ashenvale trees. [REPORTED] also volumetric fog and god
rays. The stated constraint is preserving the timeless art style. See [[Systems]].
