# Dungeons — WoW Forever

Nine new levelling dungeons, spanning roughly levels 13–60.

> [!warning] Level brackets are third-party only
> Blizzard's official ["What's Next" Panel Recap](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24303862)
> names all nine dungeons and **contains no level brackets at all**. Every bracket below comes
> from third-party transcription of the panel slides. Names, locations and story hooks are
> Tier 1; brackets are Tier 2. See [[Sources and Confidence]].

---

## The nine

| Level | Dungeon | Location | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **13–18** | Hall of Thanes | Beneath Ironforge, Dun Morogh | Alliance-centric. Resting place of the dwarven leaders of old; intruders have disturbed the tombs. "Ghostly whispers echo these corridors." |
| **15–20** | Ruins of Lordaeron | Ruined Lordaeron City, Tirisfal | Horde-centric; Alliance must sneak in. Forsaken are restoring the city; a **necromancer** has moved in, rumoured to be eyeing another city next. [REPORTED] **Scourge remnants** also still contest the ruins. Some coverage calls it "Forsaken Ruins". |
| **24–29** | Excavation Site | Wetlands, above Whelgar's Excavation | Mysterious lights, drifting mist, rogue **Titan constructs**. |
| **28–33** | City of Dalaran | Alterac Mountains | The WC3-era barrier has fallen. Malfunctioning enchantments, uncontrolled constructs, demonic activity. A wider city to explore beyond the dungeon. |
| **35–40** | The Drowned City | **Off the coast of Stranglethorn Vale** | Sunken **troll** ruins that have suddenly risen out of the ocean. **Trolls, naga and pirates** — Mills mentioned "a pirate ship crash into the place". No faction restriction. |
| **40–55** | Krol'dok Stronghold | **The Riverglades**, Eastern Kingdoms | Ogre city. An **open-world / outdoor dungeon** — which is the accepted explanation for the unusually wide 15-level bracket (likely multiple sections). |
| **48–53** | Alcaz Island Prison | Alcaz Island, Dustwallow Marsh | Where the Defias held important nobles. Previously inaccessible. |
| **55–60** | Blackmaw Hold | **Azshara** | Furbolg city behind a mysterious gate; deep tunnels, corruption stirring. **Its tunnels lead to [[Raids\|the Barrow Deeps]]** — see below. |
| **58–60** | Shaper's Terrace | Hills of Un'Goro Crater | Titan mysteries. |

## Corrections made 13 Sep 2026 (verification pass)

> [!important] Four dungeon entries changed. Do not rely on any earlier copy of this table.

**1. Krol'dok Stronghold is 40–55, not 35–40, and it is in the Riverglades.**
The 35–40 figure traces to a single source — MMO-Champion's live panel notes, the same
transcription that omitted the Lordaeron and Dalaran brackets entirely. Six independent
outlets give 40–55: Icy Veins, Output Lag (headline: *"a level 40 to 55 ogre dungeon"*),
Out of Games, wccftech, wowtbc.gg and goldgoblin. **Treat MMO-Champion's bracket as the
transcription error.**

**2. Blackmaw Hold is in Azshara. The Felwood/Winterspring theory is disproven.**
This was previously logged as a HIGH-confidence inference pointing at the
Timbermaw/Felwood/Winterspring corridor. It is wrong, and the disconfirmation is hard rather
than a vote count: **Blackmaw Hold is a pre-existing furbolg location in Azshara**, with its
own Warcraft Wiki entry and a Cataclysm-era map position in that zone. Three outlets place
the Forever dungeon there ("behind Azshara's gate"). Azshara is a 45–55 Classic zone, which
fits a 55–60 dungeon, and it borders night elf territory — which is what makes the Barrow
Deeps tunnel link below coherent. The reasoning that produced the wrong answer was sound
(corrupted furbolgs, a tunnel network, a Timbermaw-style name) and still landed on the wrong
zone, because it reasoned from lore patterns instead of checking whether the name was already
on the map.

**3. The Drowned City is off Stranglethorn Vale — but the ruins are trollish, not elven.**
The location pick was right. The lore hypothesis was not: this is not a risen fragment of
Zin-Azshari. Nora Mills described *"sunken troll ruins that have suddenly risen out of the
ocean"*, with trolls, naga and a crashed pirate ship. The Highborne/Zin-Azshari reading is
**superseded** — keep it only as a record of a discarded theory. One outlet (goldgoblin)
places it on the **Hinterlands** coast instead; two place it off Stranglethorn, and the
Stranglethorn reading is the one carried by the Mills quote. Both coasts are troll country,
so the conflict is live but minor.

**4. Lordaeron (15–20) and Dalaran (28–33) brackets are now firm.**
Both were previously recorded as soft or missing. Lordaeron 15–20 is carried by Icy Veins,
Output Lag and goldgoblin. Dalaran 28–33 is carried by Icy Veins, Out of Games, goldgoblin
and wccftech; Output Lag's 28–30 is the lone outlier and should be treated as the error.

## Location theories — status after verification

[SPECULATION] All three unplaced-dungeon theories are now resolved by evidence. This section
is retained as a record of what was inferred and how it held up, not as live speculation.

| Theory | Confidence claimed | Outcome |
|---|---|---|
| Blackmaw Hold → Felwood/Winterspring corridor | HIGH | **Wrong.** It is in Azshara, where a location of that exact name already exists. |
| Krol'dok Stronghold → inside Riverglades | MODERATE | **Correct.** The reasoning (two ogre features, same panel, same bracket band) held. |
| Drowned City → Stranglethorn coast for the pirates | LOW / working pick | **Correct on location, wrong on lore.** Troll ruins, not Highborne. |

The lesson worth keeping: the MODERATE and LOW calls landed, and the HIGH one failed. The
HIGH-confidence call was the only one that inferred a *brand-new* location from lore themes
rather than matching a name to something already on the map. Check the map for the name first.

## Open dungeon items

- Blackmaw Hold's bracket is unstated in the Out of Games transcription; 55–60 comes from
  Icy Veins, goldgoblin and wccftech.
- No official Blizzard source for **any** bracket. Beta on 17 Sep settles all nine.
- Warcraft Wiki lists **Timbermaw Hold** among dungeon locations shown in promotional
  material. No outlet reports it as one of the nine. Possibly a mislabelled Blackmaw Hold
  screenshot, possibly a tenth location. Unresolved.
