# Raids — WoW Forever

> [!important] All three raids unlock **9 December 2026**. There are no raids at launch.
> Launch is 4 November. The gap is a deliberate **five-week raidless window**.

Confirmed by the roadmap slide and carried consistently by Output Lag (headline: *"first raids
unlock December 9, Onyxia's Lair at 40 players"*), Icy Veins, Blizzard Watch and Inven Global.

---

## The three

| Raid | Size | Location | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Hyjal Summit** | **20** | Summit of Mount Hyjal | An unknown figure is siphoning energy from the mountain. Framed as **dissatisfaction with Malfurion** trading night elf immortality away to defeat Archimonde. The largest raid in Forever. |
| **The Barrow Deeps** | **10** | Night elf prison caverns | Max level. Holds some of Azeroth's most infamous prisoners. **Three entrances**; two are now located — see below. [REPORTED] a **notorious Demon Hunter** is among the prisoners, unnamed by Blizzard. Mills: *"Perhaps you'll get to see his prison."* |
| **Onyxia's Lair** | **40** | Dustwallow Marsh | The first *original* raid to open. |

Both new raids bring **new tier sets** and an **unrevealed legendary reward** ("the first new
legendary").

> [!note] Still unrevealed after the Deep Dive panel — 14 Sep 2026
> The Deep Dive covered itemization in detail — merged hit and crit, hundreds of new dungeon
> drops, expanded quest rewards, blue-quality unique boss items — and **said nothing about the
> legendary, the tier sets, or the Demon Hunter prisoner**. The What's Next phrasing, *"a
> legendary reward we're looking forward to revealing in full"*, still stands as the only
> statement on it. See [[Deep Dive Panel 2026-09-14]] and [[Systems]].
>
> **Entrance 3 also remains unlocated**, and the **Summer 2027 revamped iconic raid** is still
> unnamed.

Clay Stone (Associate Production Director) on the original raids: they *"have not opened yet"*,
with a **staged rollout** intended. He also said **"nothing is off the table, not even a new
class."**

## Barrow Deeps entrances — one now closed

[CONFIRMED] Three entrances exist. Previously only the **Mount Hyjal** tunnels were located.

**Entrance 2 is now identified: the tunnels of [[Dungeons|Blackmaw Hold]] lead to the Barrow
Deeps.** Output Lag reports this directly — *"The tunnels of Blackmaw Hold also lead here."*

This is a genuinely useful find, because it cross-validates the Blackmaw Hold location
correction in [[Dungeons]]: Blackmaw Hold sits in **Azshara**, which borders night elf
territory, so a tunnel route into the night elves' prison caverns is geographically coherent.
The Felwood/Winterspring theory would have implied a Moonglade-side entrance instead.

**Entrance 3 remains unlocated.**

## Correction log

**All three raids are on 9 December — not split.** An earlier reading had Hyjal Summit and
Barrow Deeps at launch with Onyxia's Lair on 9 Dec. That was wrong; the roadmap slide puts
all three on the same date. This correction is **re-confirmed** by the 13 Sep verification
pass against four independent roadmap write-ups.

One outlier: wccftech's summary lists a "Launch Date: November 4" alongside "New Raids:
December 9", which reads as an editing artefact rather than a competing claim. Every dedicated
roadmap article says 9 December for all three.
