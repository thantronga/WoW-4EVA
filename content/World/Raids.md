# Raids — WoW Forever

> [!important] All three raids unlock **9 December 2026**. There are no raids at launch.
> Launch is 4 November. The gap is a deliberate **five-week raidless window**.

Confirmed by the roadmap slide and carried consistently by Output Lag (headline: *"first raids
unlock December 9, Onyxia's Lair at 40 players"*), Icy Veins, Blizzard Watch and Inven Global.

---

## The three

| Raid | Size | Location | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Hyjal Summit** | **20** | Summit of Mount Hyjal | An unknown figure is siphoning energy from the mountain. Framed as **dissatisfaction with Malfurion** trading night elf immortality away to defeat Archimonde. The largest raid in Forever. |
| **The Barrow Deeps** | **10** | Night elf prison caverns | Max level. Holds some of Azeroth's most infamous prisoners. **Three entrances**; two are now located — see below. [REPORTED] a **notorious Demon Hunter** is among the prisoners, unnamed by Blizzard. Mills: *"Perhaps you'll get to see his prison."* |
| **Onyxia's Lair** | **40** | Dustwallow Marsh | The first *original* raid to open. |

Both new raids bring **new tier sets** and an **unrevealed legendary reward** ("the first new
legendary").

> [!note] Still unrevealed after the Deep Dive panel — 14 Sep 2026
> The Deep Dive covered itemization in detail — merged hit and crit, hundreds of new dungeon
> drops, expanded quest rewards, blue-quality unique boss items — and **said nothing about the
> legendary, the tier sets, or the Demon Hunter prisoner**. The What's Next phrasing, *"a
> legendary reward we're looking forward to revealing in full"*, still stands as the only
> statement on it. See [[Deep Dive Panel 2026-09-14]] and [[Systems]].
>
> **Entrance 3 also remains unlocated**, and the **Summer 2027 revamped iconic raid** is still
> unnamed.

Clay Stone (Associate Production Director) on the original raids: they *"have not opened yet"*,
with a **staged rollout** intended. He also said **"nothing is off the table, not even a new
class."**

## Raid rules — from the post-BlizzCon interviews, 14 Sep 2026

**[Tier 2]** Two mechanics that appear in **neither** panel recap, from the But Why Tho interview
with **Anna Resendez** (Lead Software Engineer) and **Clayton Stone** (Associate Production
Director). Full context in [[Post-Panel Interviews 2026-09-14]].

- **No attunement for the new raids at launch.** They mirror the original entry-level raids in
  being open on arrival. This is a real planning fact for 9 December — there is no attunement
  chain to pre-grind. Note the scope of the claim: *at launch*, and *the new raids*. Nothing was
  said about Onyxia's Lair, nor about later raids, and the spring 2027 window carries a **new
  legendary questline** that may well gate something.
- **Raid sizes are fixed — there is no flex scaling.** 10 stays 10 and 20 stays 20. Resendez:
  *"We don't wanna lean towards content tuning or automatically changing the difficulty… it's
  always gonna stay the same."* This closes off the Season-of-Discovery-style flex raiding that
  some of the community expected, and it is the same "un-solve the game" instinct behind the
  combat and itemization work in [[Systems]].
- **Encounter design favours accessibility over mechanical complexity** — Resendez described
  encounters as avoiding complex mechanics and prioritising group play rather than scaling
  difficulty.

> [!note] Interviews are Tier 2, not Tier 1
> A developer quoted by an outlet is still a third party transcribing speech. Both facts above
> are **single-outlet** as of 14 Sep. Worth a re-check when other outlets publish.

## Onyxia's head, and world buffs

> [!note] Unstated, and worth watching — added 14 Sep 2026
> **Onyxia's Lair is the only original raid with a date** (9 Dec, 40-player). In Classic, handing
> in **Onyxia's head** is what grants **Rallying Cry of the Dragonslayer**, the most valuable world
> buff in the game.
>
> **Blizzard has said nothing about the turn-in, the buff, or world buffs generally** — checked
> across both official recaps, all three post-BlizzCon interviews and every hands-on write-up. The
> source encounter is in Forever; whether the buff chain is, is unknown.
>
> **Blackwing Lair is not in the announced content**, so *Warchief's Blessing* has no source in
> Forever either way. Clay Stone's line that the original raids *"have not opened yet"* with a
> staged rollout is the only guidance on when others might appear.
>
> Full reasoning, and the adjacent design signals that point toward less buff stacking rather than
> more, are in [[Systems|Systems § World buffs and the buff economy]].

## Barrow Deeps entrances — one now closed

[CONFIRMED] Three entrances exist. Previously only the **Mount Hyjal** tunnels were located.

**Entrance 2 is now identified: the tunnels of [[Dungeons|Blackmaw Hold]] lead to the Barrow
Deeps.** Output Lag reports this directly — *"The tunnels of Blackmaw Hold also lead here."*

This is a genuinely useful find, because it cross-validates the Blackmaw Hold location
correction in [[Dungeons]]: Blackmaw Hold sits in **Azshara**, which borders night elf
territory, so a tunnel route into the night elves' prison caverns is geographically coherent.
The Felwood/Winterspring theory would have implied a Moonglade-side entrance instead.

**Entrance 3 remains unlocated.** The Deep Dive added nothing.

> [!note] A sourcing caveat on entrance 2, raised 14 Sep
> **No source uses the phrase "third entrance"**, and the two outlets carrying the Blackmaw link
> describe it differently. Output Lag states it as a route — *"The tunnels of Blackmaw Hold also
> lead here."* **Icy Veins describes the same thing as a connection between the dungeon and the
> raid, and explicitly says it is not a third entrance specifically.**
>
> This vault's reading — three entrances, of which Blackmaw is the second — is a synthesis of the
> "three entrances" statement with Output Lag's route, not something any one source says end to
> end. It remains the most economical reading and the geography supports it. But if the beta shows
> Blackmaw's tunnels as something other than one of the three numbered entrances, **this is where
> the seam is.**

## The Demon Hunter prisoner — still unnamed

**[Tier 1]** Blizzard has never named him, and the Deep Dive did not return to the subject. Nora
Mills' *"Perhaps you'll get to see his prison"* is the whole of it.

**[Tier 4 — third-party speculation, flagged not endorsed]** Icy Veins writes that the corruption
in the complex and *"an infamous demon hunter once imprisoned there"* are *"potentially hinting at
Illidan Stormrage"*, and marks it **unconfirmed** itself. Recorded so the guess is on file with its
owner attached. **It is one outlet's inference and nothing more.**

> [!danger] Do not let this become the Devourer problem in reverse
> The Barrow Deeps prisoner is an **NPC**. Forever has **nine classes and no playable Demon
> Hunters**. See the contamination register in [[Open Questions]].

## Correction log

**All three raids are on 9 December — not split.** An earlier reading had Hyjal Summit and
Barrow Deeps at launch with Onyxia's Lair on 9 Dec. That was wrong; the roadmap slide puts
all three on the same date. This correction is **re-confirmed** by the 13 Sep verification
pass against four independent roadmap write-ups.

One outlier: wccftech's summary lists a "Launch Date: November 4" alongside "New Raids:
December 9", which reads as an editing artefact rather than a competing claim. Every dedicated
roadmap article says 9 December for all three.
