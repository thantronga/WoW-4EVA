# Realms and Rulesets — WoW Forever

> [!important] New note, 14 Sep 2026
> This is the single largest reveal of the **Deep Dive panel** and it did not exist anywhere in
> this vault before 14 Sep. It also **overturns** what the vault recorded about Hardcore.
> Panel context and the full diff are in [[Deep Dive Panel 2026-09-14]].

---

## There are no realms

**[Tier 1]** *World of Warcraft: Forever* ships **realmless**. There is no server list. Instead
of picking a realm, a player picks a **ruleset**, and each ruleset behaves as a single large
ecosystem — a mega-realm.

Blizzard Watch's headline puts it plainly: *"there won't be any realms. Instead, players will be
able to choose one of four gameplay styles."* PC Gamer leads its Deep Dive write-up with the same
point: *"You pick a ruleset (e.g. normal, PvP, hardcore) — not a realm — to play on."*

**[Tier 2]** Inven Global adds that the mega-realm is served by **a channel system** for
encounters, and Warcraft Tavern speculates layering is the mechanism. Blizzard has not described
the technology; Blizzard Watch reports only the rationale — server technology now allows very
large populations in one place, so the original technical reason for splitting realms is gone.

---

## The four rulesets

**[Tier 1]** From the official Deep Dive recap:

| Ruleset | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Normal** | Baseline. Cooperation-focused, PvP by consent. |
| **PvP** | Contested territory is genuinely dangerous; ongoing world conflict. |
| **Roleplaying** | Fantasy-first, for like-minded communities. **[Tier 2]** Icy Veins and Warcraft Tavern both add *"strict naming conventions and behavior rules"*. |
| **Hardcore** | **Available after launch.** Permanent death. See below. |

A character's ruleset is chosen per character; the choice governs who they can play with.

---

## Rules that follow from being realmless

**[Tier 1]**

- **No cross-ruleset grouping.** You group with your own ruleset only.
- **Factions stay separate.** No Horde–Alliance grouping. Forever is not going cross-faction.
- **Two-part character names.** Every character gets a **first name and a last name**, unique per
  region. This is the replacement for realm-scoped name uniqueness — Blizzard Watch calls it
  simply *"surnames"*. The recap's worked example is **"Ana Forever"**.
- **Collections and Legacy Points are account-wide across rulesets**, with **Hardcore as the
  exception** (see below).
- **A language preference** determines which players you encounter in the world.

**[Tier 2 — thin]** Several outlets mention **faction balancing**, Massively OP and Wowhead
comparing the approach to **Season of Discovery**. Warcraft Tavern explicitly notes that **no
faction-balancing metric was described**. Treat "faction balancing exists" as reported and its
mechanism as **unknown**.

## Hardcore

> [!warning] This corrects the vault
> Until 14 Sep this vault recorded, in [[Roadmap]], that Hardcore would run on **separate
> official realms**. That came from What's Next coverage on 12 Sep — Icy Veins wrote that
> *"there will be official Hardcore realms"*, and the Out of Games live blog logged *"Hardcore
> realms confirmed as 'a promise'"*.
>
> **The Deep Dive panel makes that impossible as stated:** there are no realms at all. Hardcore
> is a **ruleset** inside the realmless world. Blizzard's own recap, Blizzard Watch, Warcraft
> Tavern, Icy Veins' own later piece, Massively OP and PC Gamer all describe it that way.
>
> No outlet has published a correction reconciling the two. **The earlier "Hardcore realms"
> reporting should be treated as superseded**, most likely a pre-Deep-Dive use of "realm" as a
> loose synonym for "ruleset". **No realm count has ever been given by anyone** — that part of
> the old open question is now moot rather than answered.

**[Tier 1] The ruleset**

- **Permanent death.**
- **Available after launch**, not at launch. **[Tier 2]** Output Lag reports Hardcore is **not in
  the initial beta build** either, and that no date was given at the Hardcore panel. The
  [[Roadmap]] still shows a **Winter 2026/27** season label from the roadmap slide.
- The Hardcore ecosystem is **fully enclosed**. Items, mounts and pets from other rulesets cannot
  come in.
- **Progression flows one way only.** Hardcore characters' earnings are shared out to the other
  rulesets; nothing flows back in. Blizzard Watch's gloss: *"you have to earn it."*
- **On death, a Hardcore character can transfer to another ruleset — including PvP.** Blizzard
  Watch flags the PvP destination as **new versus WoW Classic**, where that was not an option.

## The Hardcore panel — 13 Sep, 4:15 pm PDT

A separate **"World of Warcraft Hardcore: What's Next"** panel ran later the same day
(**4:15 pm PDT Sun 13 Sep = 09:15 Mon 14 Sep AEST**), about 90 minutes before this note was
written.

> [!note] Coverage is very fresh and very thin
> At the time of writing, **Output Lag is effectively the only outlet with post-panel material**,
> published as three short single-topic pieces, and its own running live post still served a
> pre-panel placeholder. There is **no official Blizzard recap for the Hardcore panel**. Every
> item below is **Tier 2 single-source** and should be re-checked once other outlets publish.

**[Tier 2 — Output Lag only]**

- **World refresh warning.** When player density in an area gets too high or too low, the game
  rebuilds the area and respawns creatures around you — historically a way to die without
  warning. Forever adds a **three-minute countdown window** with a **"move now" button** to
  confirm you are somewhere safe and trigger the refresh immediately. Principal Game Designer
  **Kris Zierhut**: *"no more are you going to lose your characters to this sudden change of the
  world around you."* Built for Hardcore, shipping **in the whole game**, and **testable in the
  beta from 17 September**.
- **Hardcore-specific Legacy challenges** arrive when Hardcore launches. Zierhut: *"there are a
  bunch of hardcore specific legacy challenges to do."*
- **Dying after level 25 still earns a Legacy point.** Zierhut frames it as *"a softer landing
  after a Hardcore death"* — the next run starts with slightly more rested experience and
  gathering. See [[Talent System]] for the Legacy Tree itself.
- **Mak'gora, off-script.** Senior Game Designer **Josh Greenfield** called Mak'gora *"a, I would
  say, unexpectedly popular part of Hardcore"* and floated a dedicated venue: *"what if we had a
  dedicated space for Mak'gora contests? Could be kind of cool. Could be something we're working
  on for a future update. Maybe."* **No date, no mechanics, no mockup.** This is a tease, not an
  announcement — file it as such.

## What is still unknown

- **The mechanism** behind the mega-realm — channels, layering, sharding. Not described.
- **Faction balancing** — reported, never specified.
- **Hardcore's date.** A season label, nothing firmer.
- **Whether "no realms" has any regional caveat.** Names are unique *per region*, which implies
  regions still exist as boundaries; nobody asked what that means for play.
